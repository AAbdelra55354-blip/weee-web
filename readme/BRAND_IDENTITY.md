# DR.WEEE Brand Identity Guidelines

## 🌟 1. Brand Essence & Story

### The Core Premise:
> *"Technology has a second life. If it cannot be measured, audited, and verified, it does not count."*

**DR.WEEE** was founded in 2018 in Cairo, Egypt by environmental entrepreneur **Essam Hashem**. Beginning as a specialized e-waste recovery workshop, DR.WEEE has expanded into an integrated group of 6 specialized operating entities across 11+ regional markets in the Middle East and Africa.

The brand operates at the intersection of **enterprise data security**, **sovereign heavy engineering**, and **audited circular commodities**.

---

## 🗣️ 2. Tone of Voice & Editorial Personality

The DR.WEEE brand communicates with the precision of an industrial engineer and the integrity of an ESG auditor.

| Dimension | How DR.WEEE Speaks | What We Avoid |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pragmatic vs Emotional** | "We weigh, sort, and refine materials to measurable purity." | "Save the cute animals and hug the trees." |
| **Operational vs Abstract** | "Serialized chain-of-custody under NIST 800-88 guidelines." | "A vague green vision for tomorrow." |
| **Authoritative vs Passive** | "We manufacture MENA's first smart industrial shredders." | "We hope to reduce waste eventually." |
| **Transparent vs Concealing** | "42% direct reuse, 53% secondary refining, 4.8% neutralized, 0.2% dust." | "100% magic zero-impact claim." |

### Terminology Standards:
- ✅ **Use**: *Circular Systems*, *Retired Technology*, *Asset Decommissioning*, *Material Yield*, *Serialized Chain-of-Custody*, *Sovereign Machinery*, *Remanufacturing*.
- ❌ **Avoid**: *Junk*, *Trash*, *Garbage dump*, *Scrap yard*, *Cheap parts*, *Green magic*.

## 🏷️ 3. Visual Identity & Logo System

### A. Primary Brand Logo
- **Asset**: `images/logos/dr-weee-logo.png` (and SVG vector master `images/favicon/favicon.svg`).
- **Composition**:
  - The circular tech emblem: Stylized green loop embodying continuous material recovery.
  - The typographic wordmark: **DR.WEEE** in bold geometric caps.
  - The descriptor: **CIRCULAR SYSTEMS** in tracked monospace below the wordmark.
- **Clear Space**: Maintain a minimum exclusion zone equal to the height of the letter "W" around the entire logo mark.
- **Minimum Digital Size**: 32px height on mobile screens; 44px on desktop navigation bars.

### B. The Brand Mascot
- **Asset**: `images/logos/dr-weee-mascot.png`.
- **Usage Rules**:
  - **Permitted**: Youth education programs, school recycling campaigns, consumer app onboarding, and community awareness social posts.
  - **Prohibited**: Corporate enterprise contracts, official ESG audit reports, Safe Recycling Certificates, and legal terms.

## 📸 4. Photography & Imagery Art Direction

Photography is a cornerstone of DR.WEEE's industrial credibility. All visuals must reflect **real physical operations**:

1. **Industrial Machinery & Sovereign Engineering**:
   - High-contrast, sharp photography of shredders, granulators, and sorting belts (`images/manufacturing/drweee-smart-shredder.jpg`).
   - Clean, well-lit factory environments highlighting Egyptian engineering excellence.
2. **Material Fractions & Macro Textures**:
   - Close-ups of hydrometallurgically separated copper pellets, gold-plated circuit board contacts, and sorted polymer granules.
3. **Operational Handoffs & Facilities**:
   - Warehouse operations showing organized barcode pallet storage, certified technicians in safety gear, and clean negative-pressure battery sorting stations.
4. **Forbidden Imagery**:
   - Never use generic stock photography showing sprout plants growing out of computer keyboards, disconnected lightbulbs in grass, or cartoonish green globes.
