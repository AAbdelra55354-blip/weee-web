Chain of Custody.

Every kilogram collected is serial-tracked, audited, and issued a verified certificate of destruction or recycling.

Scheduled pickup with tamper-evident transport bins, digital asset manifest, and signed custody transfer.

Intake & Serial Triage

Every unit is scanned, categorized by condition and metallurgy, and assigned an immutable tracking ID.

Certified Destruction

Storage media physically shredded or sanitized under DoD/NIST standards, with video evidence if required.

Official green certificates and audited ESG metrics delivered for your compliance and annual disclosures.
